Privacy policy

Heist (playheist.net) · Last updated October 24, 2026

The short version

Heist is a free online social card game played with play-money chips. To run the game smoothly, prevent fraud or cheating, and improve the gameplay, we record gameplay activity. We use two advertising tools, the Meta Pixel and the Google Ads tag, to see whether our ads on Facebook, Instagram and Google bring in players (details and how to opt out are below). We never sell your personal data for money, we never show banner ads on playheist.net, and we never see your credit card number.

What we collect

  • Your account: Your chosen display name, your email address if you register one, and the date you joined. Passwords are encrypted as a secure one-way hash which cannot be read back by anyone.
  • Your gameplay progress: Chips, coins, XP, level, unlocked cosmetic items, career win/loss statistics, skill ratings, and season milestones.
  • In-game activity: Decisions made during hands, tables joined, pack openings, drinks sent, cosmetic purchases, and chat messages (monitored solely for player safety and anti-harassment).
  • Technical telemetry: IP address, device platform, and timestamps used for server stability, anti-abuse, and network diagnostics.

Local storage

Heist uses your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage to keep you signed in, remember settings such as sound and language, keep your place at a table, and remember that you've seen the cookie notice. This stays on your device and is needed for the game to work.

Advertising and measurement tools

On playheist.net we load two tools from advertising companies. They are not anonymous: they send information about your visit to Meta or Google, who may link it to an account you have with them.

  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, for Facebook and Instagram ads). It tells Meta when a page of Heist is viewed (PageView), when you start a Heist game (StartHeist), when you create an account (CompleteRegistration), and when you finish a game (GameComplete, with the game's name and whether you won). With each event Meta's script also receives your IP address, browser and device details, the page address, and its own cookies. It sets the cookies _fbp (a browser ID, kept about 90 days) and, if you arrived by tapping a Meta ad, _fbc (the ad click ID, about 90 days).
  • Google Ads tag (Google LLC, gtag.js). It tells Google when you create an account (a sign-up conversion), finish a game, or install Heist to your home screen. Google's script also receives your IP address, browser and device details, and the page address. It can set cookies such as _gcl_au (about 90 days) and, if you arrived from a Google ad, _gcl_aw (the ad click, about 90 days).
  • Ad click IDs. When you arrive from an ad, the link carries an ID such as fbclid or gclid and campaign tags (utm_…). Heist saves these with your player record so we can see which ads bring in players who actually play.

We use this to count which ads work and to let Meta and Google show our ads to people more likely to enjoy Heist. Meta and Google handle the data under their own policies: Meta Privacy Policy and how Google uses data for ads. These tools are not loaded in the version of Heist on CrazyGames.

How to opt out of ad tracking

  • Meta: turn off "Activity information from ad partners" in your Meta ad preferences, and see Your activity off Meta technologies to disconnect past activity.
  • Google: turn off personalized ads in My Ad Center.
  • Industry opt-outs: optout.aboutads.info (US) and youronlinechoices.eu (Europe).
  • Your browser: block third-party cookies, clear cookies for playheist.net, or use a content blocker. Heist works the same with these tools blocked. On iPhone, "Ask App Not to Track" and Safari's tracking prevention also limit them.
  • Ask us: email support@playheist.net and we'll delete the ad click IDs saved with your player record.

Some US state laws (for example California's) treat sharing data with ad tools like these as "sharing" or "targeted advertising", even when no money changes hands. You have the right to opt out of that. The steps above do that today, and you can also email us to opt out.

Third-party service providers

We also use these providers to run the game:

  • Hosting: Render (render.com) runs our servers and game database.
  • Payments: if you buy something, Stripe (stripe.com) handles the payment. Stripe receives your payment details and email; Heist gets only a receipt that the payment went through, never your card number.
  • Email: messages to support@playheist.net are forwarded by ImprovMX to our inbox.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to data brokers.

Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Instant Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly in the game under Profile › Account › Delete Account.
  • Data Requests: To request a full export of your profile data or request manual erasure, email support@playheist.net.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Heist is designed for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, please notify us at support@playheist.net and we will promptly delete it.

Changes

If our privacy practices change, we will update the date at the top of this policy. For questions or privacy inquiries, contact us at: support@playheist.net.
