Privacy policy

Heist (playheist.net) · Last updated October 9, 2026

The short version

Heist is a free card game played with play-money chips. To run the game, stop cheating and make it better, we record what you do in it. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we never see your password.

What we collect

  • Your account: your display name, your email if you sign up with one, and when you joined. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, which nobody can read back.
  • Your progress: chips, coins, XP, level, items, career stats, skill rating and season pass.
  • What you do in the game: every game you play and every decision in it, purchases with coins or chips, packs opened, drinks sent, tables joined, chat messages, and which screens you open and buttons you tap.
  • Technical details: your IP address and the times you connect, which we use to keep the game running and to spot abuse.

We don't record what you type into a password field, and we don't track you on other websites.

Why

  • To run your games, keep your career on every device, and settle chips fairly.
  • To investigate cheating, harassment in chat, and disputes about a game.
  • To understand how the game is played so we can fix and improve it.

Who can see it

Other players see only your public profile: name, level, cosmetics and career stats. The game's owner can see everything listed above in a private admin panel. The game is hosted on Render (render.com), which stores the data for us. We don't sell or share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Game records and activity logs may be kept longer to resolve disputes and keep the game fair.

Your choices

  • You can delete your account at any time under Profile › Account. This clears your name, email, password and progress.
  • To ask for a copy of your data, or for your activity history to be erased, email support@playheist.net.

Children

Heist is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: support@playheist.net.
